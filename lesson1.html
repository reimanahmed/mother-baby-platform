2. Grammar: Present Simple vs Present Continuous

Present Simple (المضارع البسيط): Used for routines and permanent facts.

Example: She speaks three languages.

Present Continuous (المضارع المستمر): Used for actions happening now or temporary situations.

Example: I am studying English at the moment.
